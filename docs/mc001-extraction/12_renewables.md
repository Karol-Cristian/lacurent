## Concepts to extract

Renewable energy source:
- A source such as solar thermal, photovoltaic electricity, wind energy, biomass, geothermal/aerothermal heat through heat pumps, or renewable share in district/energy-carrier factors.
- It matters because renewable energy affects primary-energy split, RER, and sometimes delivered/final energy balance.

Delivered renewable energy:
- Renewable energy delivered to the building or system boundary by an energy carrier.
- It must be mapped to the correct primary-energy factor family before RER is calculated.

Produced renewable energy:
- Energy produced on-site, nearby, or at distance, such as PV electricity, solar thermal heat, wind electricity, or heat-pump source energy.
- Generated energy is not automatically credited; it must be allocated to self-consumption, export, or RER perimeter.

Exported energy:
- Produced energy that leaves the assessment boundary.
- MC001 handles exported electricity separately from self-consumed electricity and exported-to-grid electricity.

Self-consumed energy:
- Produced electricity used by building PEC services in the same calculation interval.
- For electricity, MC001 uses a matching factor and a `min(...)` rule.

Renewable primary energy:
- Primary-energy component calculated using renewable conversion factors.
- It must remain separate from total and non-renewable primary energy.

Non-renewable primary energy:
- Primary-energy component calculated using non-renewable conversion factors.
- It is not interchangeable with renewable primary energy.

RER / renewable contribution:
- Ratio of renewable primary energy considered for the selected perimeter to total primary energy.
- MC001 notes that RER depends on the chosen perimeter; the Romanian procedure leads to `RERdist`.

Solar thermal contribution:
- Thermal energy from active solar thermal systems serving space heating and/or DHW.
- MC001 Chapter 4.2 gives a monthly method with collector, storage, heat-exchanger, load, and climate inputs.

Photovoltaic contribution:
- Electricity generated by PV systems, allocated to PEC self-consumption, non-PEC use, export to grid, and weighted performance.
- System generation needs PV module, inverter, orientation, tilt, and solar-radiation inputs.

Heat pump renewable fraction:
- Heat pumps use ambient/geothermal/aerothermal source heat plus input energy.
- The source-energy contribution must be calculated through the heat-pump method and factor treatment, not guessed from COP alone unless the method explicitly allows it.

Biomass treatment:
- Biomass appears as an energy carrier/source whose renewable/non-renewable and CO2 behavior is determined by factor tables, not by an inline assumption.

Generated energy vs credited renewable contribution:
- Generated energy is a physical production quantity.
- Credited renewable contribution depends on allocation, perimeter, conversion factors, export treatment, and RER rules.

## Missing-input behavior

If renewable system data is missing, future calculators must return:

`status: cannot_calculate_renewable_contribution_missing_system_data`

If renewable/factor table is missing, future calculators must return:

`status: cannot_calculate_renewable_contribution_missing_factor_table`

If climate/solar irradiation data is missing, reuse:

`status: cannot_calculate_mc001_monthly_missing_climate_data`

If the electricity matching factor is required and relation (5.31) or an explicit hourly timestep is not available, future calculators must return:

`status: cannot_allocate_pv_self_consumption_missing_matching_factor`

No invented renewable production values are allowed.
