Problems we solve

When the test environment exists, but delivery is still painful.

01

Fragile automation

Unstable timing, duplicated helpers, unclear setup/teardown, brittle CAPL logic or tests that pass only under ideal lab conditions.

02

Manual qualification load

Repetitive verification, manual diagnostics checks and reporting overhead that consume engineering time and delay releases.

03

Diagnostic corner cases

UDS sessions, negative responses, recovery, wake-up behaviour, DTC state handling, debounce timing and fault-injection sequences.

04

Coverage gaps

Requirements without robust automated evidence, uneven regression depth or qualification scope that cannot scale with releases.

HIL integration friction

Bench prerequisites, hardware-state dependencies, communication setup and test logic that are too tightly coupled to manual lab operation.

06

Audit and release pressure

Need for clearer traceability, reproducible results, test evidence and a verification structure that is understandable outside the original author team.

FAQ

Typical first questions.

What does my team need to provide?

A technical owner, the selected test requirements and scripts, a baseline log, version details and authorized access to your licensed test environment. We agree the scope before starting; unavailable access pauses the delivery window.

Can you work as a specialist delivery partner?

Yes. A consultancy or HIL integrator can engage LaCurent for a bounded CANoe/CAPL diagnostic work package alongside its own delivery team.

What happens after the pilot?

We review the results and remaining work together. Further implementation or repeat regression support is scoped separately against your priorities and available delivery capacity.

How do you handle experience and confidential material?

Only professional capabilities are described here. Employer or previous customer code, requirements and test assets are never reused. Work uses your authorized inputs and agreed access arrangements. ASPICE and ASIL experience does not imply certification of LaCurent or of your product.

Can you work with an existing test environment?

Yes. The preferred starting point is often an existing CANoe/HIL setup with technical debt, incomplete automation or qualification pressure.

Do you only advise, or also implement?

Implementation is part of the offer. The goal is to deliver working automation, not only recommendations.

Can you support a short rescue engagement?

Yes. The Rescue Sprint is intentionally small so both sides can evaluate technical fit before committing to a larger scope.

Do you claim aerospace project experience?

No. Current project experience is primarily automotive embedded systems. Support for aerospace-oriented work is positioned through transferable safety-critical verification and real-time embedded engineering practices, without misrepresenting project history.

Can you work remotely with international teams?

Yes. The service is designed for remote technical collaboration with engineering teams and suppliers.
