# SHORT_TERM_VISION.md

# Short-Term Vision

LaCurent short-term goal is to become a credible home energy assessment engine.

Pe termen scurt, LaCurent nu trebuie tratat ca:

* marketplace;
* platforma de lead generation;
* aplicatie generica de recomandari AI;
* canal de vanzare pentru instalatori.

Pozitionarea corecta:

> LaCurent transforms home construction, consumption and system data into a physics-based energy assessment, then later into recommendations and market actions.

---

# Prioritate

Produsul principal este pentru locuinte.

Obiectivul este generarea unui raport energetic estimativ credibil.

Fundația este LaCurent Physics Engine.

Raportul trebuie sa arate ca un instantaneu de audit, nu ca dashboard live.

---

# Ce construim acum

1. Model fizic al cladirii.
2. Anvelopa.
3. Zone termice.
4. Necesar energetic.
5. Sisteme.
6. Energie finala.
7. Energie primara.
8. CO2.
9. Clase estimative.
10. Raport static, clar, document-like.

---

# Ce nu optimizam acum

Nu optimizam inca pentru:

* marketplace;
* instalatori;
* lead-uri;
* abonamente B2B;
* recomandari comerciale;
* preturi live;
* oferte de la furnizori.

Aceste idei raman valide, dar sunt future scope.

---

# Ordine tehnica

1. v0.1 Physical Skeleton
2. v0.2 Envelope + Thermal Zones
3. v0.3 Energy Demand
4. v0.4 Systems + Final Energy
5. v0.5 Primary Energy + CO2
6. v0.6 Classification + Reference Building
7. v0.7 Audit Scenarios

Pana la v0.6, ramanem concentrati pe metodologia de calcul si pe modelul fizic.

---

# Success metrics short-term

* number of homes modeled;
* report completion rate;
* accuracy of physics engine;
* number of validated assessment cases;
* percentage of users who understand the report;
* number of users who add real consumption/factures.

---

# Principiu de produs

The physics engine is the core asset of LaCurent. Monetization must not compromise trust in the engine.
